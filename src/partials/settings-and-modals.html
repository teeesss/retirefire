📅 One-Time Financial Events

What are One-Time Events?
These are significant financial events that happen once at a specific year. Examples include:
• Inheritance or gift received
• Large home purchase or sale (if not using Housing section)
• College tuition payment
• Wedding expenses
• Starting a business (seed capital)

How to use: Enter the year, describe the event, specify the amount, and choose whether it's income (money coming in) or expense (money going out). Click "Add One-Time Event" and it will appear in your projection for that specific year only.

🔄 Recurring Cash Flow Events

What are Recurring Events?
These are income or expenses that happen every year for a specific age range. Perfect for modeling:
• **Travel phases**: "Ages 55-65: $40k/year extra travel"
• **Part-time income**: "Ages 60-70: $25k/year consulting"
• **Education costs**: "Ages 50-54: $30k/year for kid's college"
• **Rental property income** (if not using main income fields)
• **Club memberships** or **hobbies** with ongoing costs

How to use:
1. Enter the Start Age and End Age for when this event occurs (inclusive)
2. Describe what it is (e.g., "Extra Travel Budget")
3. Enter the Annual Amount (how much per year)
4. Choose Expense (money out) or Income (money in)
5. Click "Add Recurring Event"

The planner will automatically apply this amount EVERY year from Start Age to End Age in your projection. You can add multiple recurring events to model complex spending/income phases.
